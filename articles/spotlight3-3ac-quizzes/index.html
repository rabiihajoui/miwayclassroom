English Spotlight 3 (3 AC): free live quizzes for every lesson

By Rabii Hajoui, English teacher in Morocco · 8 November 2026

I teach English in Morocco, and I made ready quizzes for every lesson of English Spotlight 3 (3rd year middle school (3 AC)). You don't need to type anything: install the book in MI WAY Classroom and start a quiz in one minute. Students answer live on their own phones, even with no internet.

What is inside

  • 26 lessons, each with a Test 1 (before you teach) and a Test 2 (after you teach), 10 questions each.
  • 5 unit tests of 30 questions to review a whole unit.
  • 4 versions of every question, so neighbours can't copy.
  • Listening for every lesson and unit, with real-sounding voices.
  • Word cards with pictures and sound, and word lists sent to phones for home.
  • Speaking activities with 🌱🌿🌳 help levels, and reading help.

How to start in one minute

  1. Install MI WAY Classroom on your Windows laptop or Android phone.
  2. In Lessons, open 📗 Ready-made lessons and add English Spotlight 3.
  3. Open Connect phones: students scan the QR code.
  4. Open a lesson and start ① Test 1.

All units and lessons

Unit 1: Tell me about your summer!

  • Welcome back: tense review, jobs & everyday situations
  • How was your summer? Summer activities & exclamations
  • I stayed home a lot this summer: past simple & -ed sounds
  • I hung out with my friends last summer: ordering past events
  • Where did you go last summer? Questions about the past
  • Tell me about a great summer day! Writing a narrative paragraph
  • unit test

Unit 2: Join the club!

  • I want to join the gaming club: school clubs & activities
  • Have you ever played the guitar? Present perfect & past simple
  • Have you ever been to Egypt? Irregular verbs & life experiences
  • What's your new friend like? Personality traits
  • I think I'm in the wrong club! Reading a comic & describing characters
  • unit test

Unit 3: Let's celebrate!

  • How do you celebrate? Celebrations & activities
  • What should I wear to the party? Suggestions
  • Would you like to come to my brother's wedding? Invitations
  • Are you going to the festival? Present continuous for arrangements
  • Celebrate Morocco! Festivals & describing a celebration
  • unit test

Unit 4: They changed the world!

  • I use it every day! Common inventions & frequency
  • People used to light their homes with candles: used to
  • The internet transformed lives! Inventors, years & numbers
  • First we had the phonograph: sequencing developments in technology
  • Would you use this invention? Opinions with would
  • unit test

Unit 5: The future is ours!

  • What future goals are important to you? Future goals & plans
  • You ought to study hospitality! Areas of study & suggestions
  • If you work hard, you'll go far! First conditional
  • I'm starting a band on Friday! Time phrases & arrangements
  • Dream big! will definitely, might & may
  • unit test
