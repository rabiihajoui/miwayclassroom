Outlook (Common Core): free live quizzes for every lesson

By Rabii Hajoui, English teacher in Morocco · 8 November 2026

I teach English in Morocco, and I made ready quizzes for every lesson of Outlook (Common Core (Tronc Commun)). You don't need to type anything: install the book in MI WAY Classroom and start a quiz in one minute. Students answer live on their own phones, even with no internet.

What is inside

  • 31 lessons, each with a Test 1 (before you teach) and a Test 2 (after you teach), 10 questions each.
  • 10 unit tests of 30 questions to review a whole unit.
  • 4 versions of every question, so neighbours can't copy.
  • Listening for every lesson and unit, with real-sounding voices.
  • Word cards with pictures and sound, and word lists sent to phones for home.
  • Speaking activities with 🌱🌿🌳 help levels, and reading help.

How to start in one minute

  1. Install MI WAY Classroom on your Windows laptop or Android phone.
  2. In Lessons, open 📗 Ready-made lessons and add Outlook.
  3. Open Connect phones: students scan the QR code.
  4. Open a lesson and start ① Test 1.
