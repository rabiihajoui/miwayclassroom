16 English classroom games: 13 on students' phones, 3 with no phones

By Rabii Hajoui, English teacher in Morocco · 8 November 2026

My students love a quiz, but after a few weeks they want something new. So MI WAY Classroom now has 13 games on students' phones and 3 games with no phones. They all use your lesson's questions, they work with 30+ students, and they need no internet.

Everyone plays: races and challenges

🪙 Coin race

Each student answers non-stop until the timer ends. Right answers earn coins to buy power-ups: double, freeze, shield, steal.

🏔️ Climb race

A non-stop race up the mountain. Right = one step up, wrong = wait 3 seconds. First to the top wins.

❤️ Survival

Everyone has 3 hearts. A wrong answer loses one. Ghosts 👻 come back with 3 right answers in a row.

💰 Treasure chests

A right answer opens a treasure chest: gold, steal, swap… or lose! The richest student wins.

🐉 Boss battle

The whole class against a monster. Every right answer hits it. If most of the class is wrong, it attacks!

Team games

👥 Team quiz

Teams answer the lesson questions on their phones. A team scores when its members are right.

🔐 Escape room

Teams solve puzzles on their phones. Each right answer opens a lock and gives a letter of the code word.

🧩 Team race

Teams of 3. The answers are split between the phones, so nobody can answer alone: the team must talk.

🔍 Detective mystery

Groups of 4 solve a case. Each phone has different clues: students read them aloud and find who did it. 8 ready mysteries included.

Speaking, writing and creative games

🕵️ Impostor

Secret impostors hide in the class. Everyone answers to fix the ship, then the class talks in English at the meeting and votes.

🤥 Fake answer

Everyone writes a fake word for a lesson sentence, then finds the real one. Fool your classmates to win.

🎨 Draw & guess

Draw a sentence from the lesson, the next student guesses it, the next draws the guess… Then laugh at the books and vote.

🤔 Two truths and a lie

Everyone writes 2 true sentences and 1 lie for your task. The class votes to find the lie. No lesson needed.

No phones? Use the projector

🔥 Hot seat

One student sits with their back to the screen. The team describes the word in the picture without saying it.

🎯 Board game

Numbered squares on the board. Teams take turns: behind each square is a question or a surprise.

🎡 Spin the wheel

Spin to pick a student who is here, a question from a lesson, or anything from your own list.

Is it safe?

Yes. Before the projector shows a sentence that a student wrote, you check it (✓ or ✗). Games are practice: they never count as a test. Everything stays in your classroom: no accounts, no cloud.

How to start

  1. Open 🎲 Activities & games.
  2. Choose 📱 Games with phones or 🙌 Games without phones.
  3. Choose a game and a lesson. Each game starts with a short How to play, so students understand fast.
  4. At the end, give the winners a 👍 (+1 bonus) if you want.
