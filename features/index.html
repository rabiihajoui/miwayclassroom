What's new · Version 1.0.32 · November 2026

New in MI WAY

Free updates for every teacher. The app asks you to update by itself.

New

💡 Why, 😎 How sure? and 🗣 Talk & answer again

After each answer, the board shows the right sentence and a short rule. Students say how sure they were, so you see who was sure but wrong. When the class is split, each student talks for 1 minute with the student next to them, then answers again.

New

🔁 Warm-up: do you remember?

Start class with 5 quick questions from lessons a week ago and 2+ weeks ago, mostly the class's weak skills, in new versions. The board shows what students remembered: “75% now, it was 64% last time”.

New

📝 Global Tests in one click

After every 2 units, print the Global Test of each class: 2 full pages /20 (comprehension, language, writing), Test A and Test B, and an answer page with a model writing. It tests only the lessons the class played. Print it or open it in Word.

New

🎧 Listening for every lesson

A listening for each lesson and each unit in all 9 books, with real voices (young voices for students). Students answer on their phones, with 🌱🌿🌳 help. Word cards: tap a key word to read and hear its meaning.

New

🎮 8 new class games

Impostor, Draw & guess, Fake answer, Team race, Hot seat, Speed chat, Two truths and a lie, Detective mystery. Before each game, an owl 🦉 explains how to play, with a practice turn.

New

🧠 Skill map

See each skill of each student over the year: 🟢 good, 🟡 almost, 🔴 needs help. Every phone practises its own weak skills at home.

And also

  • ✍️ Writing check: the laptop finds mistakes, you send hints, then a mark /20
  • 🗣️ Speaking check: students read aloud, the laptop listens (offline)
  • 📲 MI WAY Student app for Android: pair once, find the teacher on any Wi-Fi
  • Aa Reading help: big text, dyslexia font, read aloud, extra time
  • 🖨️ Printables: worksheets, bingo, crosswords, flashcards and certificates with your signature
  • 📱 MI WAY Remote for Android: start tests, run the quiz, take attendance and pick a student from your phone
  • 📱 Words to learn at home
  • 📒 Record book and lesson plans
