What's new · Version 1.0.12 · October 2026

New in MI WAY

Free updates for every teacher. The app asks you to update by itself.

New

🎧 Listening for every lesson

A listening for each lesson and each unit in all 9 books, with real voices (young voices for students). Students answer on their phones, with 🌱🌿🌳 help. Word cards: tap a key word to read and hear its meaning.

New

🎮 8 new class games

Impostor, Draw & guess, Fake answer, Team race, Hot seat, Speed chat, Two truths and a lie, Detective mystery. Before each game, an owl 🦉 explains how to play, with a practice turn.

New

🧠 Skill map

See each skill of each student over the year: 🟢 good, 🟡 almost, 🔴 needs help. Every phone practises its own weak skills at home.

New

📱 Words to learn at home

Send each student the words of their level. They study on the phone, even offline. With 80% they level up: 🌱 → 🌿 → 🌳 → 🏆.

New

📒 Record book and lesson plans

The app writes each lesson for you: objectives, what you did, marks /20, attendance and notes. Print it or export to Excel. Smart lesson plans with ready-to-start activities.

New

👨‍👩‍👧 Reports for parents

One report per student: attendance, progress, marks, skills and how to help at home, in Arabic. Send it on WhatsApp in one click, print it, or show it on the student's phone.

  • Aa Reading help: big text, dyslexia font, read aloud, extra time

    Questions

    Does it really work with no internet?

    Yes. Your phone's hotspot or your laptop makes a small local network. Students connect to it. No mobile data is used.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No ads, no paid version, no limit on students. One optional feature, AI quiz drafting, needs your own paid Claude key.

    What do students need?

    Any phone with a browser: Android or iPhone. No app and no account.

    I don't use Moroccan textbooks. Can I still use it?

    Yes. You can type your own lessons for any book and any level.

    Is my students' data safe?

    Everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent to a cloud. Privacy policy

    Does it work on Mac or iPhone for the teacher?

    Not yet. The teacher needs an Android phone or a Windows laptop. Students can use any phone.
