How it works

Ready in 1 minute

  1. 1

    Turn on your hotspot

    Or use the school Wi-Fi. The internet is not needed.

  2. 2

    Students scan one QR code

    They tap their name. No app to install, no account.

  3. 3

    Start the quiz

    Answers arrive live on your screen or projector.

Features

Everything an English class needs

Ready for your textbook

Spotlight 1AC–3AC, Outlook, Ticket to English, Gateway and Insights. Or make your own lessons for any book.

Students love it

Points, a podium, sounds and confetti. Every question has 4 versions, so neighbours can't copy.

Listening and speaking for every level

A listening for every lesson and unit, with real-sounding voices, plus speaking activities. 🌱🌿🌳 help levels, so every student can take part.

Vocabulary with pictures

Word games in class, word cards students can read and hear, and word lists sent to phones to study at home.

All your class tools

Pick a student, attendance, seating plan, behaviour points, record book with marks /20, Excel export.

Private by design

Everything stays on your device. No cloud, no tracking, no student accounts.
